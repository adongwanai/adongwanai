# Brand Assets

Icons are sourced from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- [Baidu](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/baidu.svg): original path, brand-blue fill.
- [Lenovo](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/lenovo.svg): original path, brand-red fill, viewBox fitted to the wordmark.
- [WeChat](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/wechat.svg): original path, green fill.
- [TikTok](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/tiktok.svg): shared musical-note mark for the Douyin link, red fill.
- [Xiaohongshu](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/xiaohongshu.svg): original path, red fill.
- [GitHub](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/github.svg): original path, gray fill for light and dark backgrounds.

Homepage and community glyphs are from [Tabler Icons v3.34.1](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/tree/v3.34.1/icons/outline), under the [MIT license](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/v3.34.1/LICENSE). Original world and users-group paths, with blue and amber strokes and accessible titles. The glyphs are navigation symbols, not organization logos.
